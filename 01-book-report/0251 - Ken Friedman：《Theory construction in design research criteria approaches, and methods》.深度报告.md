3. **52.6%**：(6) The central concepts of a theory must be well defined and must harmonize as much as possible with similar concepts of enlightened discourse.
4. **88.4%**：For this reason, the misguided effort to link the reflective practice of design to design knowledge, and the misguided effort to propose tacit knowledge or direct making as a method of theory construction must inevitably be dead ends.
5. **94.3%**：‘Experience alone, without theory, teaches . . . nothing about what to do to improve quality and competitive position, nor how to do it’ writes Deming<sup 20</sup (p 19) in his critique of contemporary manufacturing. ‘If experience alone would be a teacher, then one may well ask why are we in this predicament?
